## Overview

Unstable Core is a 2D platformer where the player controls a robot with a malfunctioning energy core. Energy continuously rises over time, and if it reaches 100, the player experiences a meltdown and dies.

The player must manage both Health and Energy while fighting enemies, using temporary boosts, and navigating environmental hazards to reach the exit.

## Controls

* A / D - Move
* Space - Jump
* J - Attack
* K - Block / Parry
* E - Speed Boost
* Q - Attack Boost
* R - Jump Boost
* F - Drone Ability

## How It Was Built

The idea behind this game was to make the player constantly feel under pressure from an unstable energy core. I built systems such as Energy management, combat, boosts, a drone companion, and environmental hazards to support this idea. The game was developed in Unity using C# and focuses on balancing Health and Energy while trying to reach the exit.


## How to Play

* Defeat enemies to restore Health.
* Monitor the Energy meter and prevent it from reaching 100.
* Use boosts to gain temporary advantages.
* Use the drone companion to recover Health and reduce Energy.
* Avoid hazards and reach the exit to complete the level.
